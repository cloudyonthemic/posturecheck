Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Posture Check ("the app") is built to be private by design. We do not operate servers, we do not require accounts, and we do not collect your personal data.

What data the app handles

  • Posture check-ins, streaks, and settings are stored on your device. If you are signed into iCloud, this data syncs through your private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit. We (the developer) cannot access, read, or export this data — it belongs to your Apple account.
  • Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not see or store your payment information.
  • Notifications are scheduled locally on your device.

What we do NOT do

  • No analytics that identify you, no advertising identifiers, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling, sharing, or transferring data to third parties.
  • No account creation, email collection, or contact information.

Health disclaimer

Posture Check provides general wellness reminders and exercise suggestions. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Consult a healthcare professional for persistent pain or medical concerns.

Data deletion

Deleting the app removes all local data. To remove iCloud-synced data as well, delete the app's data from iCloud in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Changes
