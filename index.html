Common questions

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Open the iOS Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Posture Check. Cancelling stops future charges; you keep access until the end of the current period.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
Open Posture Check → Settings → Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID.

Does my data sync between devices?
Yes — through your private iCloud. No account needed. Sign into the same iCloud on both devices.

Reminders stopped arriving?
Check iOS Settings → Notifications → Posture Check is allowed, and that a Focus mode isn't silencing them during your desk hours.
